About This Product

Ultra Penetrating Oil is Vinnie's flagship wood treatment. Its uniquely small molecular structure allows it to penetrate deeper than any comparable product on the market — without solvents. Once inside the wood, it polymerises and hardens the fibres from within while remaining flexible, so it moves with the timber rather than cracking.

The UPO acts as the tree's natural resin — providing lasting internal protection against moisture, dirt and wear. Paired with Hard Wax or Eternal Seal, it forms Vinnie's complete natural 2K system. Coverage: one 270ml can treats 25–30m².
