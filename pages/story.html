Origin & Philosophy

The Vinnie's Oil Story

How four Swedish master woodworkers set out to create the world's strongest, most natural wood protection system — and why it belongs in Australia.

Our Products & Philosophy

A System Inspired by Nature

Vinnie's Oil is a series of toxic-free and VOC-free hardwax oils for wood, made from high-quality cold-pressed, polymerising oils with naturally small molecules for better penetration — combined with hard waxes controlled for purity, forming a complete system that protects timber the way a living tree protects itself.

"We want to think in the same way when creating our treatments — looking at how nature handles its challenges on a molecular level, and trying to learn from it and copy it."

The tree has its own natural 2K solution: resin and bark, each with different functions that make timber last for hundreds of years — with no synthetic chemicals required. The solution is already made by nature, and always seems to be optimal once you understand the full picture.

Take plastic as a counterexample: a great protective material, until we realise it can also harm us through microplastics, hormone imbalances, toxicity, and environmental damage. The same applies to many conventional wood preservatives — incredibly effective, but at the cost of human health and environmental integrity.

We believe there is a better way. Nature has already solved the problem.

The Natural 2K System

Resin and Bark — Reimagined

Vinnie's Oil is built around the same principle a living tree uses to survive for hundreds of years. Two complementary products, two complementary functions.

Ultra Penetrating Oil (UPO)

The tree's resin

Penetrates deep into the wood fibres, hardening from within while remaining flexible so it won't crack as timber moves. Provides lasting internal protection against moisture, dirt and wear.

+

Hard Wax or Eternal Seal

The tree's bark

An outer shield that protects against moisture, dirt and everyday wear. The most concentrated hard wax on the market — or our invisible Eternal Seal barrier coating.

Together, UPO + Hard Wax (or Eternal Seal) form Vinnie's natural, toxin-free 2K system — the strongest wood protection available without compromising on safety or environmental impact.
The Origin

Sweden, 2021 — Where It All Began

For thousands of years, Swedes have possessed extensive knowledge about wood and how to preserve it — from Viking longships to today's modern woodworking culture.

In the deep forests of Sweden in early 2021, four master woodworkers came together with a shared frustration. The products on the market were either far too toxic — exposing users and their families to harsh chemicals — or they simply didn't provide the level of protection and durability that professional craftspeople demand.

They wanted to create a wood treatment that was perfect in every aspect: outstanding appearance, long-lasting protection, and completely free from solvents and toxins. One that wouldn't yellow the wood or require re-application every year to stay effective.

"We had no interest in creating a typical, ordinary hardwax oil — our goal was the strongest toxic-free hardwax oil for wood that nature has to offer."

1

The Problem Identified

Four Swedish master woodworkers recognise that every available product is either too toxic or too weak. They decide to build something better.

2

Relentless Testing

The team subjects formulas to extreme stress tests — including their legendary "untrimmed test": washing, scrubbing and soaking end-grain boards in red wine and coffee for over 30 minutes.

3

The Formula Found

Using nature's own logic — small-molecule cold-pressed oils and pure hard waxes — they achieve deep molecular bonding without solvents, toxins, or compromise.

4

Vinnie's Oil Launched

The strongest toxin-free system for wood is released in Sweden and rapidly gains recognition among professional woodworkers and craftspeople.

5

Arriving in Australia

THJP Enterprises Pty Ltd becomes the exclusive authorised distributor of Vinnie's Oil across Australia and its Territories — bringing the Swedish standard to Australian timber.
