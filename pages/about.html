A New Chapter

Vinnie's Oil Australia

The Australian chapter of the global Vinnie's Oil family — two best mates, a deep respect for timber, and a product that finally matched their values.

Our Products & Philosophy

Protecting Timber the Right Way

Vinnie's Oil Australia is part of the global Vinnie's Oil family, bringing a premium Swedish-made and developed natural wood protection system to Australian conditions. Our products are a carefully developed range of toxin-free, VOC-free hardwax oils made from high-quality cold-pressed, polymerising oils.

These oils contain naturally small molecules that penetrate deep into timber, working from within rather than just coating the surface. Combined with high-purity hard waxes — free from paraffins and unwanted additives — they form a complete system that protects timber against moisture, wear, and everyday use.

Together, they function much like a living tree's own defence system — resin and bark — protecting from the inside out.

Inspired by Nature

A tree survives for hundreds of years using a simple but powerful system: resin penetrates and strengthens from within, while bark protects externally from the elements. This natural "2K system" works without synthetic chemicals, toxins, or shortcuts — and so does Vinnie's Oil.

Toxin-Free Living

We believe you shouldn't have to choose between protecting your timber and protecting your health. Vinnie's Oil delivers both.

Sustainable Solutions

Nature-inspired formulas that respect the environment, reduce toxic exposure, and prioritise long-term sustainability over short-term convenience.

Long-Lasting Protection

Products that genuinely protect timber for the long term — not just on paper, but under the extreme Australian conditions of UV, humidity, and heavy use.

Preserving Natural Beauty

Timber should look like timber. Our finishes enhance the natural grain, colour and character of wood — they never mask or yellow it.

The Australian Story

How Vinnie's Oil Came Down Under

Vinnie's Oil Australia began with two best mates who shared a deep respect for timber and a passion for craftsmanship. Working with wood day in and day out, they saw the same problem time and time again — products that either performed well but were full of harsh chemicals, or were marketed as "natural" but didn't hold up in real-world conditions.

In Australia, where timber faces everything from intense UV radiation to coastal humidity and heavy domestic use, this gap was even more obvious and frustrating.

When they discovered Vinnie's Oil, it immediately clicked. The philosophy — nature-first, toxin-free, high-performance — aligned perfectly with their own values. It wasn't just another product; it was a system that respected timber the way they did.

They knew it belonged here. So they brought Vinnie's Oil to Australia — adopting it as a product ready for local conditions, local timber species, and the way Australians live and work with wood.

Vinnie's Oil Australia is operated by THJP Enterprises Pty Ltd as the exclusive authorised distributor for Vinnie's Oil products across Australia and its Territories.

Why Australia Needs Vinnie's Oil

  • Harsh UV conditions degrade conventional finishes faster than anywhere else
  • Coastal humidity requires a finish that breathes without trapping moisture
  • Australia's unique timber species benefit from deep molecular penetration
  • Growing demand for toxin-free products in homes and commercial spaces
  • Professional woodworkers need fast cure times without sacrificing quality
  • Food-safe certification critical for kitchen surfaces, cutting boards and dining furniture
