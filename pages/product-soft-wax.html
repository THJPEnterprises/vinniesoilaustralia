About This Product

Vinnie's Soft Wax provides quality surface protection with an easier, more forgiving application compared to the Hard Wax. It is ideal for maintenance coats on previously treated surfaces, refreshing worn finishes, and applications where a softer, more workable wax is preferred.

Like all Vinnie's products, the Soft Wax is 100% toxin-free, VOC-free and food grade. No solvents, no harsh chemicals, no unpleasant odours. Just a natural, effective wax that extends the life of your timber surface and keeps it looking its best.
