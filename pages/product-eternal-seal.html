About This Product

Eternal Seal is Vinnie's topcoat barrier — the tree's 'bark' in the natural 2K system. Applied over UPO once it has penetrated and begun curing, Eternal Seal forms a tough, invisible outer layer that repels water, oil, dirt and everyday surface abuse.

Unlike traditional lacquers, Eternal Seal remains breathable and natural-looking. It enhances the wood without adding a plastic-like sheen. Paired with UPO, the two products together deliver the most comprehensive toxin-free wood protection system available anywhere in the market.
